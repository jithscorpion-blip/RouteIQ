# RouteIQ First Pilot Data Loading Guide

## Pilot scope

Start small:

- 1 depot
- 5 vehicles
- 5 drivers
- 20 to 50 customers
- 50 to 150 orders
- 5 to 10 planned routes

## Loading order

1. Depots
2. Vehicles
3. Drivers
4. Customers
5. Orders
6. Routes
7. Route stops
8. Stop-order links

## Data rules

- Use anonymized customer names for staging.
- Validate all customer coordinates before routing.
- Keep Google Routes disabled until route count and quota strategy are confirmed.
- Verify vehicle capacity fields before lock/release testing.
